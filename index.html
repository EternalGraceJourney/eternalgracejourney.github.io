Our heart

Why Eternal Grace Journey exists

We believe the second half of life isn't a closing chapter — it's a season of harvest and handing on. Eternal Grace Journey offers Christian encouragement, room for quiet reflection, and a simple way to tell the people you love what God has done in your life.

“Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.”MATTHEW 11:28 · KJV
Is this only for people over fifty?

Not at all. It's written with older adults in mind, but grown children who want to capture a parent's stories are just as welcome.

How should I use the free guide?

Pick one question. Write in a notebook, talk it through with a grandchild, or print the guide. You never need to answer them all.

Can I buy a journal here?

Not yet. The journals are still being prepared, and this site doesn't take any payments.

Where are my reflections saved?

Only in this browser, and only when you tap “Save on this device.” Nothing is sent to us. Anyone using this browser could see it, so you can export or delete it any time.

Can I get weekly encouragement by email?

Not yet — we're working on it and will share here when it opens.

Five Faith Stories Your Family Should Hear

Choose just one. Write it in a notebook, tell it over dinner, or record it as a voice memo.

  1. Who first helped you understand God's love? What do you remember most about them?
  2. What hard season taught you to trust God more deeply?Share only what feels comfortable.
  3. Which prayer has stayed with you — answered, unanswered, or still unfolding?
  4. What family memory reminds you that God has been faithful?
  5. What do you most hope the next generation will know about your faith?

A conversation starter: “Can I tell you about something that happened when I was about your age?”
